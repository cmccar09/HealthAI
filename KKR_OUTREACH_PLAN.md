---

### **"How is this different from what MultiPlan does?"**

**Response:**
*"MultiPlan optimizes claim PRICING after data is already extracted. We solve the upstream problem - getting clean data OUT of medical documents in the first place. Think of us as creating the clean input data that MultiPlan's algorithms need. Natural integration opportunity with your portfolio."*

---

### **"What's preventing Epic/Cerner from building this?"**

**Response:**
*"Three things: One, they're walled gardens focused on their own data. Two, we're vendor-neutral - work with ALL EHR systems. Three, we're AI-native architecture vs. their 20-year-old systems. We actually see Epic as a distribution partner via their App Orchard marketplace."*

---

### **"Regulatory risk - is this FDA regulated?"**

**Response:**
*"We've consulted with healthcare regulatory counsel. As a document processing tool for ADMINISTRATIVE purposes (billing, claims), we're not a medical device. If we moved into clinical decision support, that would trigger FDA review. We're deliberately staying administrative-focused to avoid 18-month approval cycles."*
