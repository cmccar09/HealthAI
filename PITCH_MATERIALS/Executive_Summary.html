HealthAI - Executive Summary

AI-Powered Medical Document Processing Platform

The Problem: $68B Annual Waste in Healthcare Documentation

Healthcare wastes $68 billion annually on medical billing errors caused by manual document processing:

  • Health insurers process 5 billion claims/year at $18-25 per claim, with 30% requiring manual review
  • Manual extraction takes 5 minutes per page with 30% error rates
  • Claims processing costs $5+ per page for manual work
  • Billing errors delay reimbursement by 7-14 days

Our Solution: Automated Medical Data Extraction

HealthAI uses advanced LLM AI to automatically extract structured clinical data from any medical document with 96% physician-validated accuracy in 2 seconds per page.

Upload any medical PDF to see real-time extraction of:

  • Medications with dosages and frequencies
  • Diagnoses with ICD-10 codes
  • Lab results with normal ranges and flags
  • Procedures and imaging reports
  • Automatic document categorization
