## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

### Things That Kill Deals:

1. **No medical validation** - "How do you know it's accurate?"
2. **Regulatory uncertainty** - "Is this FDA-regulated?"
3. **No customers** - "Who's using it today?"
4. **Team gaps** - "No healthcare experience on team?"
5. **Unclear GTM** - "How will you sell to hospitals?"
6. **Poor unit economics** - "AWS costs are 40% of revenue?"

### How to Address:

1. Get 3 physicians to review 1,000 extractions
2. Prepare FDA regulatory memo from law firm
3. Convert pilots to paid ASAP (even $5K/month)
4. Recruit healthcare advisor immediately
5. Detail account-based sales strategy
6. Optimize costs before fundraising (show 70%+ margins)
